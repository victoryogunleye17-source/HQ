HQ · Runs entirely in your browser, nothing is uploaded anywhere · Two on-device models: COCO-SSD (80 categories, gives positions/boxes) + MobileNet (1000 ImageNet categories, whole-image, catches things COCO-SSD's narrow vocabulary misses) · Even together, an object outside both vocabularies will still be misidentified — that's a hard limit of running vision fully offline in a browser
